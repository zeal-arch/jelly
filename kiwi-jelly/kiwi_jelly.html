Inside the experiment

Tetrahedral Soft Body

The kiwi wedge is discretized into a 3D volumetric tetrahedral mesh generated from a 2D polar-hex Delaunay lattice extruded across height layers. Physics are computed with XPBD on a fixed 60 Hz step with 10 substeps.

Co-rotational per-tetrahedron shape matching preserves the shear elasticity, while hydrostatic volume constraints enforce gelatinous incompressibility, producing natural lateral bulging when compressed or stretched.

Optical Scattering & Anatomy

Volumetric shaders compute per-tap Beer–Lambert transmission through the flesh, scattering in the lobed cream core and 56 radial rays, wet glossy reflections over the embedded teardrop seeds, and velvet rim sheen on the fuzzy outer skin.
