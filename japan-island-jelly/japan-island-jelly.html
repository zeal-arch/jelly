How it's made

Torii at High Tide is a procedural coastal diorama. Drag to explore the shrine, touch the water, or change the season and afternoon light.

  • Weathered architecture: Grain, patchy paint, roof texture and dark tidal marks give the timber a lived-in surface.
  • Japanese pine: Irregular crowns, fine foliage detail and seasonal color sit beneath warm sun and cool sky light.
  • Living water: The gate and grove reflect in the rippled surface, while gentle wavelets break along the changing tidal flats. Reflections can be switched off in Tune.
  • Your view: Explore four seasons, adjust water clarity, or switch canopy styles in Tune.
